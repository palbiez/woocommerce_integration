Anforderungen:

Erstellung einer Integration von Etsy und Woocommerce
Soll auf separatem Linux Server bei hetzner laufen

Aufgaben: 
List WooCommerce products on Etsy via both automatic and manual options.
Keep track of orders and shipping information across WooCommerce and Etsy marketplace.
Keep your stock and orders updated automatically to prevent overselling and bad customer experience.
Access accurate and quick product upload on Etsy with category mapping and template profiling.
Avoid duplicate listings and manage your stock easily by linking products through SKUs.
Import all your Etsy listings in your WooCommerce store and manage all products from a single place.
Direct and bring Etsy buyers to your WooCommerce to retarget and nurture buyer relationships.
